Starting October 31, Saturdays are community days where all are welcome to join in the meal offering and/or attend the public Dhamma talk afterwards. Anyone who would like to make an offering is welcome to join. Since daylight saving time is ending, the schedule is earlier than before; please aim to offer food around 10:30 AM.

The schedule is as follows:

**10:30 AM** - Community Meal Offering

**11:45 AM** - Public Dhamma Talk

**Location:** 2414 30th Ave, San Francisco, CA

Saturday talks continue through November 21.
