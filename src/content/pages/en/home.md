Welcome to our community space for those interested in the monastic path, supporting Buddhist monastics living like the Sangha at the time of the Buddha, and following the journey of Venerable Jeffrey Hancuff in particular.

Venerable Jeffrey Hancuff (Bhante Guṇavīro, often addressed respectfully as "Bhante") has returned to the Bay Area and is now staying at 2414 30th Ave, San Francisco, CA.

Through November 24, he is available for drop-in Q&A on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM, and on Saturdays all are welcome to the Community Meal Offering at 10:30 AM followed by a public Dhamma talk at 11:45 AM.

In late November or early December, Venerable Jeffrey will be going on retreat. If you'd like to participate in meal offerings during the retreat, please join our WhatsApp group by emailing [samanasevana@gmail.com](mailto:samanasevana@gmail.com).
