Welcome to our community space for those interested in the monastic path, supporting Buddhist monastics living like the Sangha at the time of the Buddha, and following the journey of Venerable Jeffrey Hancuff in particular.

Venerable Jeffrey Hancuff (Bhante Guṇavīro, often addressed respectfully as "Bhante") has returned to the Bay Area. He is available for Q&A in San Jose until October 24, and starting October 25 will be at 2414 30th Ave, San Francisco, CA, with drop-in Q&A hours on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM through November 13.
