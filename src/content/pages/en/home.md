Welcome to our community space for those interested in the monastic path, supporting Buddhist monastics living like the Sangha at the time of the Buddha, and following the journey of Venerable Jeffrey Hancuff in particular.

Venerable Jeffrey Hancuff (Bhante Guṇavīro, often addressed respectfully as "Bhante") has returned to the Bay Area and is available for Q&A from October 5 until October 25 at 1215 Curtner Ave, San Jose, CA 95125.
